Contensis is an enterprise-level Content Management System (CMS) developed by Zengenti, designed to help organizations create, manage, and deliver digital content across multiple platforms and devices through HTTP, JavaScript, and .Net APIs.

**Areas:** CMS · Content · Headless CMS · Content Management · Digital Experience Platform · Content Delivery · Webhook · Higher Education

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Contensis: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
